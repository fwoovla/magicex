Story Ideas for "House of the Last"

Setting:
In a post-apocalyptic world where magic flows through the remnants of ancient battles, a tavern stands as a sanctuary amidst chaos. 
The land is scarred by factions that misunderstand or misuse magic, leading to conflict. Your character awakens in this isolated tavern, 
saved by the owner, who is determined to restore balance.


Story Overview

You are a member of "The House of the Last," a dwindling faction of guardians dedicated to preserving ancient knowledge of magic and the 
fallen wizards. The tavern’s owner, a former member of the House, tasks you with reigniting the beacons that once protected the realm 
and summoned the ancestors to vanquish the purists who threaten peace.



Gameplay Mechanics
Skills

Players can choose skills upon leveling up that affect their playstyle:

    Brewing: Create powerful potions using found mushrooms, enhancing your abilities and strategic options.
    Cooking: Prepare meals that provide buffs and can be sold for trade.
    Tracking: Hunt for resources and locate hidden secrets or enemies.
    Crafting: (No!) Not available, adding a limiting factor to force players to explore for items rather than creating them.

Passive Skills

Passive skills enhance base stats as players progress:

    Stamina/Recovery: Increases endurance and accelerates health regeneration.
    Health: Boosts maximum health pool.
    Movement: Enhances speed and agility for dodging or exploration.
    Strength: Increases melee damage and carrying capacity.

Experience Points (XP)

XP can be gained through:

    Completing quests provided by the tavern owner and other NPCs.
    Defeating enemies, especially those guarding ancient ruins or mushroom sites.
    Discovering hidden areas and lore-rich ruins.

Economy and Trade

The economy revolves around trade value, with items like potions, meals, and crafted gear having fluctuating worth. Players must 
strategically manage resources and negotiate with NPCs or other players.



Magic and Equipment

Magic is prevalent but often misunderstood:

    Wands and Staves: Primary weapons that carry magical spells and can be imbued with charms or mods to increase specific stats 
    (e.g., damage, cooldown reduce).

    Dead Wands: Found in ruins, these wands can be "pressed" to imbue spells or powers.

    Mushrooms: Found at old battle sites, used for brewing potions that grant temporary buffs, poison defenses, or recharge wand power.

Mushroom Varities

Mushrooms vary in potency, influenced by the brewing skill level. Some examples:

    Redcaps: Provide minor health regeneration.
    Poison Caps: Boost poison defense.
    Guardian Mushrooms: Enhance cooldown recovery.

Quests and Progression

After awakening in the tavern, players engage in quests such as:

    Basic Protection: Help the owner fend off bandits who threaten the tavern.
    Gathering Resources: Venture into nearby ruins or old battle sites for mushrooms and items.
    Beacons Quest: Travel to distant towns to reactivate beacons, dispelling the purists and granting safety from prying eyes.

Main Conflict

The purists have turned off the beacons to hide their nefarious activities of enslaving "un-pures." As players successfully reignite the beacons, 
they will summon the ancestors—powerful beings that once ruled, leading to confrontations and ultimately a climactic battle to reclaim the land.


Additional Features

    Fast Travel: Use a map on the wall in the tavern to unlock and fast travel to explored sites, facilitating exploration and quest progression.

    Environmental Storytelling: Lore-rich areas yield spells, enchanted items, and crucial backstory to the world, allowing deeper immersion.

    Character Creation: Players start by defining their background and skills, choosing how they relate to the broader conflict.











    Spellpress Overview

Spellpress serves as a crucial mechanic in "House of the Last," allowing players to imbue blank wands with spells. The quality and 
potency of the spells transferred depend on several factors, including player skill, the quality of the spellpress, and the nature 
of the spells being used.


Spellpress Mechanics
Pressing Spells

    Blank Wands: Obtainable through exploration, these wands serve as the foundation for imbuing spells.
    Spell Transfer: Players can press spells into blank wands using the Spellpress, transforming them into offensive or defensive tools.

Factors Affecting Spell Quality

    Player Skill Level: Higher Spellpress skill increases the quality of the imbuement, yielding wands with enhanced stats.
    Quality of Spells: Spells of higher rarity or power result in stronger effects when pressed. Common spells yield 
    lower-quality wands, while rare spells produce top-tier wands.


Stat Range Variability

The result of pressing a spell into a wand comes with a specific range of attributes, which can include:

    Damage Output: The amount of damage a spell deals.
    Cooldown Time: How quickly the spell can be used again.
    Mana Cost: The amount of mana required to cast the spell.
    Stat Modifier: Percentage increases (or decreases) for attributes like critical chance or elemental affinity tied to the spell.

Upgrading Spellpress Quality
Scavenging Components

To improve the quality of the Spellpress, players can gather rare components from:

    Ruins: Explore abandoned towns, battle sites, and ancient wizard towers to find specialized components such as:
        Arcane Crystals: Enhances the spell’s magical affinity.
        Urns of Sealed Energy: Increases the overall potency of the Spellpress.
        Essence of Ancients: Tied to the land's magical heritage, boosts the skill quality.

    Specialty Traders: Certain NPCs or traveling merchants offer unique components exclusively for upgrading the Spellpress. These can include:
        Enchanted Tools: Improve the effectiveness of the Spellpress.
        Crafted Additives: Allow for one-time boosts when imbuing spells, guaranteeing better results for specific pressing actions.


Practical Implications in Gameplay

    Exploration: Players will be encouraged to explore for both blank wands and components, fostering a deeper interaction with the game world.
    Strategic Choices: Players can specialize in certain spells, leading to diverse playstyles based on their Spellpress abilities.
    Economic Earnings: Enhanced wands can be sold or traded, creating a robust economy where players can profit from their crafting prowess.

By integrating these mechanics, "House of the Last" will provide players with a dynamic and rewarding experience, allowing them 
to create personalized, powerful wands that reflect their unique choices and skills. This deepens the narrative of rebuilding 
a world where knowledge and magic intertwine.


Spell System for "House of the Last"

In "House of the Last," spells are categorized into offensive and defensive types. Each spell has unique properties, levels, and 
can be pressed into wands to enhance their capabilities. Below is a structured overview of the spell system, including categories, 
types, and examples of unique spells.

Offensive Spells
Spell Sources

    Force
    Fire
    Lightning
    Poison

Spell Types

    Pistol: Quick, single-target spells.
    force bolt, fire bolt, lightning bolt, poison bolt

    Machine Gun (MG): Rapid-firing spells with moderate damage.
    force rain, fire rain, lightning rain, poison rain

    Grenade: Area effect spells that deal damage to multiple targets.
    force smash, fire ball, chain lightning, contaigous gas

    Instant Area Effect: Immediate damage in a defined area.
    force fist, inferno burst, storm, toxic cloud

Unique Offensive Spells

    Force Bolt
        Type: Pistol
        Effect: Deals base damage that pushes enemies back.
        Level Progression: Damage increases by 10% per level.

    Inferno Burst
        Type: Grenade
        Effect: AoE fire damage with a chance to ignite enemies.
        Level Progression: AoE radius and damage increase by 15% per level.

    Lightning Strike
        Type: MG
        Effect: Rapid lightning bolts that deal chain damage to multiple targets.
        Level Progression: Increases chain target limit by 1 per level.

    Toxic Rain
        Type: Instant Area Effect
        Effect: Deals poison damage over time in a designated area.
        Level Progression: Damage per tick increases by 5% per level.

Defensive Spells
Spell Sources

    Simple Defense
    Spell Effect Defense
    Health Drain
    Recharge Effects

Unique Defensive Spells

    Barrier Shield
        Source: Simple Defense
        Effect: Absorbs a percentage of incoming damage.
        Level Progression: Absorption percentage increases by 2% per level.

    Reflective Aura
        Source: Spell Effect Defense
        Effect: Reflects a portion of damage back to attackers.
        Level Progression: Reflection percentage increases by 1% per level.

    Vitality Drain
        Source: Health Drain
        Effect: Steals health from a target and heals the caster.
        Level Progression: Health stolen increases by 5% per level.

    Mana Recharge
        Source: Recharge Effects
        Effect: Restores a portion of mana over time.
        Level Progression: Mana restoration rate increases by 1% per level.

Spell Levels and Enhancements

Each spell has five levels, which incrementally increase their potency:

    Level 1: Base spell stats.
    Level 2: Damage or effectiveness increase based on the level's stats.
    Level 3: Further increases in potency and range.
    Level 4: Near-max efficiency with added effects.
    Level 5: Maximum stats and effects, providing the strongest version of the spell.

Example of Spell Pressing

When pressing a spell, the final stats will take into account:

    Base Spell Damage: The initial damage of the spell.
    Level Damage Increase: Each level provides incremental enhancements.
    Skill Modifier: A bonus applied based on the player's skill in Spellpress.

For example, a Force Bolt Level 2 pressed onto a wand might deal:

Damage Calculation:

    Base Damage: 30
    Level 2 Damage Increase: 30 * 10% = 3
    Skill Modifier: +5 (based on player’s skill)

Total Damage: 30 + 3 + 5 = 38

This structured spell system adds significant variety and strategic depth to "House of the Last," enabling players to tailor 
their magical arsenal to fit their playstyle while fostering exploration and skill progression. The balance between offensive 
and defensive spells ensures players can adapt to various challenges, encouraging them to explore and master their abilities.
